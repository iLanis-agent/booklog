BookLog

"I'll finish it this weekend" - said about every book since March. Readers are optimists, and nobody tracks what they actually read per day.

BookLog is honest math for your reading life. Log the page you're on whenever you read; after two check-ins it knows your real pace and projects the date you'll actually finish each book - per book, side by side.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
